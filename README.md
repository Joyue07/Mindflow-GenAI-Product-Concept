# Mindflow-GenAI-Product-Concept
*Academic group project at Stockholm University.*

## Overview
MindFlow is a conceptual multimodal training platform that explores how Generative AI, real-time biofeedback and immersive VR could be combined to support focus training in high-pressure academic and professional situations. 

The project focused on translating user needs into a product concept, testing the concept through iterative prototyping, and evaluating its potential value and business model.

> **Note:** This project is a conceptual prototype and was not developed as a fully functional production system.

## Problem
Through interviews with students and professionals, we identified recurring challenges around maintaining focus in demanding environments, understanding personal cognitive states, and transferring focus strategies into real-world situations.

The project reframed these challenges into a design question:
*How might we design a safe system that helps users understand and actively regulate their focus under pressure?*

## Design Process
The project followed a Design Thinking process:
**Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test**

### Empathize & Define
Interviews were conducted with students and professionals to explore high-pressure situations, existing coping strategies and unmet needs.

### Ideate
Generative AI was used as a brainstorming partner to generate and narrow down potential concepts. More than 20 ideas were explored before converging on the MindFlow concept.

### Prototype
The concept evolved through three stages:
- Paper prototype for interaction logic
- Wizard of Oz simulation using existing VR and wearable technology
- High-fidelity conceptual prototypes for the VR interface and feedback experience

### Test
A preliminary survey with 15 undergraduate and graduate students was used to evaluate perceived value, feature acceptance and practical concerns.

## Concept
MindFlow consists of three main components:
- **Diagnostic Engine:** Visualizes focus-related performance through a capability radar chart.
- **Training Engine:** Uses GenAI and real-time physiological signals to adapt simulated high-pressure scenarios.
- **Digital Anchor:** Explores how a physical gesture could be associated with a trained focused state and recalled in real-world situations.

## Prototype Evolution
One important design change occurred during prototyping. 

The initial concept used a numerical dashboard to provide real-time feedback. During simulation, the team identified that continuously monitoring numbers could itself become distracting. The design was therefore changed toward **Ambient Feedback**, using subtle environmental cues instead of persistent numerical information. 

This iteration illustrates how prototype testing influenced the interaction design.

## User Evaluation
The preliminary survey involved 15 target users. Key observations included:
- **80%** expressed strong interest in a visualized cognitive ability radar chart.
- **73%** highly recognized the potential value of the Digital Anchor concept.
- **67%** indicated willingness to try the service on a paid basis.
- Hardware comfort and motion sickness were identified as important concerns.

*These findings were treated as early concept-validation signals rather than evidence of market validation at scale.*

## Business Model
The project used Lean Canvas to explore the business potential of the concept. The proposed model combines:
- Upfront hardware sales
- Recurring SaaS subscriptions
- Personalized GenAI training modules

The analysis also considered customer segments, value proposition, channels, costs and key metrics.

## Ethical & Societal Considerations
The project considered several issues related to physiological data and AI-enabled cognitive training, including:
- Privacy and data protection
- Potential misuse of user data in workplace settings
- Safety limits for high-stress training
- Avoiding interpretation of the system as a general intelligence assessment

These considerations were incorporated into the proposed future development of the concept.
